Class Connect - Privacy Policy

Developed by Hasham Rasheed (Kzynovex) • kzynovex.online • Updated: October 2026

1. Solely for Educational Purposes

Class Connect is an academic utility built as a class project to assist university and college students with weekly timetable tracking, class cancellation notifications, and peer-reviewed study notes sharing.

2. Minimal Information Collection

We believe in absolute student privacy. We collect only what is strictly necessary to identify your student account within your cohort:

  • Google Identity (Name & Email): Retrieved securely via Google OAuth 2.0 to display your account profile.
  • Academic Roll Number: Provided by you to link with your class section and prevent duplicate accounts.
  • Study Materials: Voluntary PDF lecture slides or study summaries you submit for class peer review.
Privacy Guarantee: We do NOT collect your device location, contact books, financial data, or advertising identifiers. We NEVER sell, share, or monetize student information.

3. Permissions Required & Justification

Permission Reason Required
Internet Access Sync live timetable schedules, notifications, and notes with Supabase.
Notifications Alert students 10 minutes prior to lecture start and announce emergency room changes.
Storage / Media Allow students to pick lecture notes, assignment PDFs, or past exams from device storage.
Camera (Optional) Snap photos of whiteboard formulas or handwritten notes to upload into the study vault.

4. Academic Content Policy

Students and contributors must strictly refrain from uploading personal photographs, confidential documents, or sensitive personal data. The Academic Vault is reserved exclusively for verified curriculum materials.
